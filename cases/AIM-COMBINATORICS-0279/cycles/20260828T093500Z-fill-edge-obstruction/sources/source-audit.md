## Existing structural dependency

Maria Chudnovsky and Paul Seymour, Even-hole-free graphs still have bisimplicial vertices.
https://web.math.princeton.edu/~pds/papers/bisimplicial2/paper.pdf
Displayed revision 26 June 2024; Theorem 1.1 and its following paragraph, printed page 1. The paper states the bisimplicial-vertex result and explains the invalid clique-transfer step in the older proof. Its full proof remains unverified by this packet. It does not assert that degree-two deletion orders are width-two filled orders.

## Treewidth context and novelty boundary

Kathie Cameron, Murilo V. G. da Silva, Shenwei Huang and Kristina Vuskovic, Structure and algorithms for (cap, even hole)-free graphs.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.08066v1
Version 1, 24 November 2016. Section 5 defines treewidth through triangulations and states a width-five bound for triangle-free odd-signable graphs. Page 3, Figure 1 discussion, already notes a triangle-free even-hole-free graph with a K5 minor. That stronger published obstruction is not reconstructed here. The new repository artifact is the smaller explicit fill-edge diagnostic, not a novel structural bound.

## Unused lead

Layered-wheel search results distinguish (theta, triangle)-free graphs from (even hole, K4)-free graphs. They do not justify an unbounded-treewidth claim for triangle-free even-hole-free graphs. No such claim is used. No search snippet substitutes for the primary pages above.
