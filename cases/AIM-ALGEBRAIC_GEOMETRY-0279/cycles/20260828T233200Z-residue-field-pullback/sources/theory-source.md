# Primary sources — theory lane

Accessed 2026-08-28. [Stacks Section10.52](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00IU),
Lemma10.52.3 gives length additivity. Lemma10.52.12 weights length under local
restriction by residue-field degree. Lemma10.52.6 identifies length with
residue-vector-space dimension for modules annihilated by the maximal ideal.
These support the two ends of our exact-sequence argument; ideal equality is
derived separately. No flatness assumption is imported into this application.

[Huneke, Hilbert-Kunz multiplicity and the F-signature, v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.0467v1),
Introduction and Section3, defines Frobenius powers and local module-length
normalization. This is the normalization used here, not raw F2-dimension of
a quotient whose residue field is K. Its universal results are not independently
reproved by the finite experiment.

[Brenner–Monsky, Tight closure does not commute with localization, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.2913v2),
Section2, treats a characteristic-two quartic family and algebraic parameters.
It is a scope reference, not evidence that the node model is a Monsky quartic
or that the imported descending sequence has been verified.
