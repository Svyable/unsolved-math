# Primary-source audit — accessed 2026-08-28

[Stacks tag 01XS](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01XS), Lemmas 30.8.1
and 30.8.4, give the P1 twist and projective-bundle direct-image formulas.
Applying them to E=O plus O(n) requires the explicit divisor conversion in
theory.md; they are not mirror-symmetry statements.

[Hochenegger v4, March 1, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.16553v4), Examples
2.7 and 4.6, use C_+=S+nF. Example 2.7 identifies the canonical divisor as
(n-2)F-2C_+, but Example 4.6 includes that divisor in its immaculate list.
This discrepancy is recorded, not silently corrected. The version also has an
erratum concerning Proposition 5.13; that is a different claim and is not
invoked to resolve this discrepancy.
