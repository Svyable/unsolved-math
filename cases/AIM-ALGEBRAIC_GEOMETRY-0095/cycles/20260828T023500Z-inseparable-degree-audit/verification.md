# Independent-method verification

## Counterexample-first procedure

The verifier was authored and run before theory.py existed. It first checks
the characteristic-two rank-one maps with exponents 2 and 4, a degree-one
control, an off-diagonal map, and the degree-three extension-field control.
It then reconstructs all specified cases and only afterward reads the theory
certificate. The baseline has no access to a theory-output argument.

## Distinct algorithms

For each exponent matrix, the verifier builds the subgroup generated by its
rows in (Z/ac)^2 by a queue search. Every coset is explicitly labeled.
Element orders are obtained by repeated addition until the identity coset.
The p-primary and prime-to-p subgroup sizes independently recover the two
degree factors; their product is checked against the number of cosets.
The modulus ac is an input-derived common annihilator, justified by the
adjugate identity ac*Z^2 subset Lambda, not by trusting a supplied table.

Jacobian rank uses Gaussian elimination over F_p. Rational kernel counts
enumerate all pairs of exponents modulo p^r-1 for r=1,2. This models the
cyclic multiplicative groups; it is not a finite-field library computation.
Theory instead uses gcd/Smith invariant factors, p-adic factor extraction,
lcm order histograms, and closed kernel formulas.

All 648 cases match, including 1,296 kernel counts.
The proposed p^(2-rank) shortcut fails in 98 cases. Rational kernel counts
are below total degree in 1,023 of the 1,296 observations.
Seven altered/deleted fields or rows are rejected by full reconstruction
comparison. These are specified mutation controls, not general security
claims. Replays reproduce identical output bytes.

## Proof-step audit

The finite quotient enumeration certifies the specified integer lattices.
Its interpretation as function-field degree still uses the Laurent
monomial independence argument. Separability uses power-map factorization,
not just derivative rank. Nonzero determinant modulo p is sufficient for
separability; lower rank alone measures only a first-order defect.

For the explicit p=2, a=4,b=0,c=1 case, the lattice quotient has four
elements all of 2-power order, and Jacobian rank 1. The pure fourth-power
map is bijective on the points of every finite extension of F_2, while
its function-field degree remains 4. The p=2 degree-three control separates
missing rational roots from inseparable multiplicity.

The field-diagram check is a separate reasoning audit: K_C=K makes the
evaluation inclusion identity, regardless of the other inclusion.
K_B(t)=k(x1,...,xn) has the required transcendence degree, and its extension
to K is finite by the chosen basis. Proper models and the covering-family
equivalence are a source dependency; no smooth-pencil assumption is added.
This is not an independently authored proof of the general bridge.

## Citation audit and objections

See sources/verification-source.md for the separately inspected PDF
definitions and theorem locator. The source's separability condition is
on evaluation, not on the finite map whose degree is minimized.
The supplied AIM URL is unavailable. No status or omitted source text was
inferred from that failure.

Both implementations and notes have same-assistant authorship and share the
frozen specification. Independence is process/algorithm/evidence independence,
not independent human/model/kernel review. All five successful mathematical
child processes use Python -I, an empty environment and resource limits;
there were no failed mathematical invocations. No network calls occur in
the scripts; process isolation is not a network namespace.

The ambient surface is rational; these chosen maps need not minimize degree.
No geometric lower-bound example, minimum irrationality computation,
finite-extension attainment proof or very-general/countable-field
specialization theorem was produced. Imported statuses remain unchanged.

Reproduce: python experiments/run.py baseline, or python experiments/run.py replay.
The baseline, verifier output and execution logs are distinct verification
evidence; they do not reuse theory evidence paths.
