## Known geometric model

Wouter Castryck and John Voight, Nondegenerate curves of low genus over small finite fields.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.2060v1
https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.2060
Author-hosted copy: https://jvoight.github.io/articles/nondeg_lowgenus-final.pdf

Version 1 is dated 12 July 2009 in arXiv metadata; the PDF also carries a typesetting date of 21 August 2018. These dates are not equated. Main theorem, printed page 2, identifies the genus-three model C2:
(x+y)^4=(xy)^2+xy(x+y+1)+(x+y+1)^2 over F2.
Moving all terms to the left and homogenizing gives exactly this packet's F. Section 4, printed page 7, discusses its extension-field point counts. The packet independently recomputes them; no novelty, classification or maximality claim is made.
